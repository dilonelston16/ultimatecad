ULTIMATECAD — STORE, INVENTORY, AND MARKETPLACE OVERHAUL

1. Extract into:
   C:\Projects\ultimatecad

2. Run:
   supabase/migrations/202608060012_store_inventory_marketplace_overhaul.sql

3. Build:
   cd C:\Projects\ultimatecad
   rmdir /s /q .next
   pnpm build

4. Push:
   git add .
   git commit -m "feat: overhaul stores inventory and marketplace"
   git push origin main

WHAT THIS ADDS
- New /inventory route showing items, vehicles, weapons, properties, and businesses.
- Marketplace dropdowns only display assets actually owned by the active character.
- Server-side ownership validation prevents fake asset listings.
- Store groups: Government, General Stores, and Business Stores.
- Product categories inside every store.
- Business store purchases route revenue to the business operating account.
- Vehicle, weapon, property, item, service, and document product types.
- Purchases of vehicle/weapon/property products create actual owned records.
- Large government catalogue of GTA vehicles, licensed weapons, and properties.
- Founder/admin restock controls for system stores.
- owner_purchase_restriction foundation is included but disabled by default.
  Later, set it true on selected general stores to restrict purchases to active
  business owners.
