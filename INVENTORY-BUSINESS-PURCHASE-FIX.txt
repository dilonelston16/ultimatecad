ULTIMATECAD — INVENTORY QUANTITY + BUSINESS PURCHASE FIX

WHAT WAS WRONG

1. Personal purchases inserted new rows every time, so identical items appeared
   separately instead of as one item with a quantity.

2. Business purchases could roll back while crediting the selling business
   account. The buyer was incorrectly required to have management permission
   over the seller's business.

3. There was no Use Item action.

WHAT THIS PATCH DOES

- Consolidates existing duplicate personal inventory rows.
- Consolidates duplicate business inventory rows.
- Stacks future personal purchases by character and product.
- Stacks future business purchases by business and product.
- Repairs the seller-credit transaction flow.
- Ensures business purchases appear in Business > Inventory.
- Keeps the Add to Store action working.
- Adds Use Item, which removes one unlisted quantity.
- Prevents using quantities reserved for marketplace listings.

INSTALL

1. Extract into:
   C:\Projects\ultimatecad

2. Run:
   supabase/migrations/202608060014_inventory_quantity_business_purchase_fix.sql

3. Build:
   cd C:\Projects\ultimatecad
   rmdir /s /q .next
   pnpm build

4. Push:
   git add .
   git commit -m "fix: stack inventory and repair business purchases"
   git push origin main
