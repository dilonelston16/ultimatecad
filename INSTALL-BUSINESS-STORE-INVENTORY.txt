ULTIMATECAD — BUSINESS, STORE, AND INVENTORY COMPLETION

Built directly against uwncad(5).zip.

1. Extract into C:\Projects\ultimatecad and replace included files.
2. Run in Supabase SQL Editor:
   supabase/migrations/202608060013_business_store_inventory_completion.sql
3. Build:
   cd C:\Projects\ultimatecad
   rmdir /s /q .next
   pnpm build
4. Push:
   git add .
   git commit -m "feat: complete business stores and inventory"
   git push origin main

WHAT THIS PATCHES
- Inventory & Assets added to sidebar.
- Business creation restored.
- New businesses automatically receive bank account, storefront, inventory, and categories.
- Existing businesses are repaired with storefronts/categories.
- Stores support Personal or Business purchase destination.
- Business purchases debit business account and enter separate business inventory.
- Owners can publish business inventory into their store and set category/price.
- Owners can add custom store categories.
- Business store sales continue routing into the business account.
- Expanded clothing, business supply, construction, farming, fishing, camping,
  automotive, electronics, and fictional RP contraband catalogue.

NOTE
The contraband entries are fictional roleplay records only and contain no real-world
manufacturing or use instructions.
