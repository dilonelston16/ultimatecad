ULTIMATECAD — BUSINESS MANAGEMENT COMPLETION

This update removes duplicate business creation from Economy and completes the
business operations page.

INSTALL

1. Extract into:
   C:\Projects\ultimatecad

2. Run in Supabase SQL Editor:
   supabase/migrations/202608060009_complete_business_management.sql

3. Build:
   cd C:\Projects\ultimatecad
   rmdir /s /q .next
   pnpm build

4. Push:
   git add .
   git commit -m "feat: complete business banking and employee management"
   git push origin main

WHAT CHANGED

Economy:
- Removed the duplicate Register Business form.
- Added one Manage Businesses link.
- Economy remains the financial overview and loan area.

Businesses:
- Overview, Banking, Employees, and Payroll tabs.
- Business bank balance and account number.
- Business-to-account transfers.
- Business transaction history.
- Hire by State ID.
- Fire and rehire.
- Promote and demote.
- Change role and pay during promotion/demotion.
- One-time employee payments.
- Payroll draft creation and processing.
- Employee checking-account validation.
- Immutable business transaction ledger.
