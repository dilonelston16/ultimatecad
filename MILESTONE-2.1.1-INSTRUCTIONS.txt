ULTIMATECAD MILESTONE 2.1.1 — BUSINESS REPAIR

WHY THE BUSINESS PAGE FAILED
The previous page attempted to filter businesses through an embedded
business_members relationship inside one PostgREST .or() query. That query is
not reliable and can fail before the page renders.

THIS PATCH
- Loads owned businesses and employee memberships separately.
- Loads accounts, employees, and stores separately.
- Combines those records on the server.
- Repairs missing owner memberships.
- Repairs missing business operating accounts.
- Restores owner RLS access to businesses, employees, and business accounts.
- Shows database errors on the page instead of silently redirecting.

INSTALL

1. Extract into:
   C:\Projects\ultimatecad

2. Run:
   supabase/migrations/202608060007_repair_existing_businesses.sql

3. Build:
   cd C:\Projects\ultimatecad
   rmdir /s /q .next
   pnpm build

4. Push:
   git add .
   git commit -m "fix: repair registered business loading"
   git push origin main

AFTER DEPLOYMENT
Open:
   /businesses

The business you already registered should display with its:
- Business number
- Operating bank account
- Balance
- Employees
- Stores
- Ownership status
